How the picks are made

Each day the model estimates every team’s chance of winning from season team stats and days of rest. That estimate is compared with the win chance implied by the sportsbook’s moneyline, after removing the sportsbook’s margin. A pick appears when the model sees at least a 2% edge.

The stake shown is a quarter of the Kelly fraction, which assumes the model’s probabilities are right. They may not be. Every pick is logged before tip-off and graded afterward, wins and losses alike.

How to read the bar: the blue fill is the model’s win probability for the pick. The dark tick is the sportsbook’s implied probability with the vig removed. The gap between them is the edge.
